# Privacy Policy for ScoreMate

Last updated: May 9, 2026

ScoreMate is an offline scorekeeping app. The app is designed to work without an account, login, or backend service for normal app use.

## Data Collection

The developer of ScoreMate does not collect personal data from the app.

ScoreMate does not:

- Require a login or user account
- Use a backend server for normal app use
- Show ads
- Track location
- Collect personal data for the developer

## Local App Data

ScoreMate stores app data locally on your device so the app can keep track of your games. This may include:

- Player names
- Match names
- Scores
- Round history
- Selected theme
- Selected language

This data stays on your device unless you choose to export it, share it, or otherwise send it outside the app.

## Sharing and Exporting

ScoreMate may allow you to share match results or export app data using your device's share options. When you choose to share or export data, that data is handled by the app or service you select, such as email, messaging, notes, cloud storage, or another sharing destination.

Only share or export data if you are comfortable sending it to the selected destination.

## Third-Party Services

ScoreMate does not use ads, location tracking, or a developer-operated backend for normal app use.

If you install ScoreMate from an app store, the store provider may process information related to app download, purchase, updates, crash reporting, or device diagnostics according to that provider's own policies.

## Data Control

Because ScoreMate stores scorekeeping data locally, you control the data on your device. You can remove app data by using any clear/reset options provided in the app or by uninstalling the app from your device.
